About Densbe

More than 40 years of extensive regional experience across the Asia Pacific.

Our company

Built by people from the electrical industry

Established in 1983, Densbe Electrical has been delivering more than 30 years of extensive regional experience in the Asia Pacific regions. We offer procurement and maintenance (EPCM) services to the Oil & Gas (Onshore & Offshore), Petrochemical, Petroleum, Semiconductor, Medical and Construction industries.

Densbe Electrical Pte Ltd has grown to have the capability and expertise to provide you with the complete range of electrical materials. These include, but are not limited to, Explosion Proof Electrical Equipment, LED Lightings, Industrial Fans, Special Lamps and Commercial Lighting. We extend our services to include value-added services to become a technology-driven solution for clients.

Densbe Electrical Pte Ltd strives to provide short lead time with ex-stock and highly responsive support for you and your direct clients. With our dedicated maintenance arm, we are also able to provide prompt after-sales support.
